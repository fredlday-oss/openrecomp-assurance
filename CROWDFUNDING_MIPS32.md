# OpenRecomp MIPS32 crowdfunding campaign

The OpenRecomp MIPS32 hardening campaign is live on Crowdfunder:

**https://www.crowdfunder.co.uk/p/openrecomp-mips32**

## Assurance relevance

The campaign funds hardening and validation of OpenRecomp's existing MIPS32 path. `openrecomp-assurance` is relevant because it records and challenges bounded evidence across source artifacts, translation, execution, replay and observable comparison.

Current assurance evidence includes bounded real MIPS32 and bounded real MIPS32 ELF-ingestion gates. Those results remain fixture-bounded and do **not** establish arbitrary MIPS32 compatibility.

Wider/general MIPS32 support therefore remains **CANDIDATE**.

**Funding does not make MIPS32 PROVEN. Evidence does.**

The funded programme targets:

- ingestion and extraction hardening;
- MIPS32 semantics and translation;
- bounded indirect-control-flow evidence;
- native and WebAssembly parity/repeatability;
- rights-safe fixtures and negative tests; and
- public documentation and evidence.

Existing work will not be rebilled as new campaign work.

## Rights and reproducibility

Public campaign evidence will use synthetic, open, homebrew, public-domain or otherwise authorised material as appropriate. The campaign does not fund distribution of copyrighted games, proprietary firmware, console keys or proprietary SDK material.
